# GP32emu

Based on [gameblabla/gp32emu](https://github.com/gameblabla/gp32emu).
This fork is maintained at [gajae1/gp32emu](https://github.com/gajae1/gp32emu).

The GP32 and S3C2400 hardware model includes work derived from MAME's
Game Park GP32 driver by Tim Schuerewegen under BSD-3-Clause.
The included `licenses` files provide third-party attribution and terms.

The iOS frontend uses UIKit, CoreGraphics, AVFoundation and GameController.
BIOS images, games and saves are supplied by the user and are not bundled.
